# RedFace Skool product constitution

**RedFace Skool** is the product people use. **Refesco** is the marketplace line.

RedFace Skool is **marketplace infrastructure**, not a school.

## Positioning

> A technology-driven learning marketplace where people discover knowledge, learn from industry experts, attend events, build professional portfolios, and connect with opportunities.

Not: an online school where teachers teach students.

## Two-sided market

| Students | Instructors |
|----------|-------------|
| Discover courses | Publish courses |
| Buy learning | Earn revenue |
| Attend events | Host events |
| Follow experts | Build reputation |
| Certificates of completion | Verified profile |
| Learning identity / portfolio | Sell knowledge |

RedFace Skool sits in the middle. It does not employ instructors.

## Three learning types

1. **Courses** — self-paced programs with a price.
2. **Live learning** — workshops and sessions on a date.
3. **Community / events** — talks, hackathons, networking. Some free so members stay without buying a course.

## Titles (never professor)

Instructor, educator, lecturer, expert, mentor, industry professional, course creator.

## Verification (instructors)

- Unverified
- Identity verified
- Professional verified
- Credential verified
- RedFace Skool expert

Uploading a PDF is not the same as Credential verified.

## Certificates

- **RedFace Skool Certificate of Completion**: this platform, this course.
- **Industry certification**: AWS, CompTIA, etc. issued by that body. RedFace Skool may offer a **prep pathway**. It does not issue their credential.

## Ecosystem loop (architecture, not v1 scope)

Learn → Practice → Build → Certify → Network → Work

v1 ships Learn + Network (profiles, events) + Pay. Practice/Build/Work come later without a second payments stack.

## Payments

All money movement is **RedFace Pay**. See PAYMENTS.md.

## Language

Merchant-facing and student-facing copy: no kernel, rail, orchestrator. No em dashes in UI.
